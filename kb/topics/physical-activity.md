# Physical Activity

> **Worked example** of a cross-cutting domain page: the same evidence
> is reachable from here and from the condition pages it touches
> (hypertension today; diabetes, obesity, depression as they're added).
> Cited sources are `unverified` until checked.

## Current practice snapshot

- Adult prescription: 150–300 min/wk moderate or 75–150 min/wk
  vigorous aerobic activity plus muscle-strengthening ≥2 days/wk; no
  minimum bout length; some is better than none; sit less
  (src-hhs-2018-physical-activity-guidelines).
- Condition-specific payoff, blood pressure: aerobic exercise lowers
  SBP ~5–8 mmHg in hypertension (src-acc-aha-2017-htn) — see
  `topic-hypertension` for the full BP picture.
