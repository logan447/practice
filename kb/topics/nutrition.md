# Nutrition

## Current practice snapshot

- Core prescription across guidelines and the LM board canon: a
  plant-predominant pattern of minimally processed vegetables, fruits,
  whole grains, legumes, nuts, and seeds; global guidelines most
  often encourage vegetables/fruit/whole grains and discourage
  red/processed meat and refined grains (board-lm-nutrition, Obj 3,
  [LMBR refs 131, 1]).
- Strongest pattern-level evidence: Mediterranean (PREDIMED primary
  prevention; Lyon secondary prevention), DASH (BP), Portfolio (LDL
  ~−29%, statin-comparable), MIND (cognition) — citations resolved in
  `kb/boards/lifestyle-medicine/nutrition-refs.md`.
- Quantitative anchors for counseling: fiber 25/38 g/d; added sugar
  AHA ≤24/36 g; sodium ≤2,300 (ideal <1,500) mg; saturated fat 5–6%
  of calories; ~70% of sodium arrives in processed food
  (board-lm-nutrition, Obj 1–2).

## Where sources disagree

- WHI's low-fat Diet Modification trial showed no cancer/CVD
  reduction at 8 years, while pattern-based trials (Lyon, PREDIMED)
  succeeded — the book frames this as total-fat% being the wrong
  target vs food pattern. Keep both when counseling about "low-fat."
