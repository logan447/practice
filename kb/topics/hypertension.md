## Current practice snapshot

- Screen all adults ≥18 with office BP; confirm out of office (home BP
  or ABPM) before diagnosing or treating (src-uspstf-2021-htn-screening,
  src-acc-aha-2017-htn).
- Diagnostic categories: stage 1 at 130–139/80–89, stage 2 at ≥140/≥90
  (src-acc-aha-2017-htn).
- Stage 1 → drugs only when clinical CVD or 10-yr ASCVD risk ≥10%;
  otherwise lifestyle first. Stage 2 → drugs + lifestyle. Target
  <130/80 for most treated adults (src-acc-aha-2017-htn).
- Intensive control in high-risk non-diabetic adults reduced CV events
  and mortality, at the cost of hypotension/AKI/electrolyte events, and
  with the AOBP measurement caveat (src-sprint-2015).
- Lifestyle levers with approximate SBP effects: DASH diet ~11 mmHg,
  aerobic exercise ~5–8 mmHg, sodium reduction ~5–6 mmHg, weight loss
  ~1 mmHg/kg, alcohol moderation ~4 mmHg (src-acc-aha-2017-htn).
