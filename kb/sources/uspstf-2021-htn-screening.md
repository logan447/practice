# USPSTF 2021 — Hypertension Screening in Adults

> **Worked example, seeded from Claude's training knowledge —
> `unverified` until checked against uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org
> (also confirm it is still the current statement).**

## What it says

- **Grade A:** screen for hypertension in adults ≥18 with office blood
  pressure measurement.
- Obtain BP measurements **outside the clinical setting** (ABPM or
  home BP) for confirmation before starting treatment.
- Suggested intervals: annually for adults ≥40 or at increased risk;
  every 3–5 years for adults 18–39 at low risk with prior normal BP.

## Population & scope

Community-dwelling adults without diagnosed hypertension; screening,
not management (management → src-acc-aha-2017-htn).

## Caveats & exceptions

- Reaffirmation of the 2015 statement; evidence review focused on
  detection accuracy and confirmation strategies.

## Context

Screening (USPSTF) and diagnosis/management thresholds (ACC/AHA) come
from different bodies — keep the task distinction when synthesizing.
