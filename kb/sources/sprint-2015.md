# SPRINT (2015)

> **Worked example, seeded from Claude's training knowledge —
> `unverified` until checked against the NEJM paper.**

## What it says

- RCT, n≈9,361: intensive SBP target <120 vs standard <140 mmHg.
- Stopped early (median follow-up ~3.3 yr): primary composite (MI,
  other ACS, stroke, HF, CV death) reduced ~25% (HR ≈0.75); all-cause
  mortality also lower in the intensive arm.
- More serious adverse events in intensive arm: hypotension, syncope,
  electrolyte abnormalities, acute kidney injury — not injurious falls.

## Population & scope

Excluded diabetes, prior stroke, significant proteinuria,
polycystic kidney disease, nursing-home residents. BP measured with
automated office protocol (AOBP) — readings run lower than routine
office measurement, which matters when translating <120 to practice.

## Caveats & exceptions

- The AOBP measurement-technique caveat is the classic exam and
  journal-club point: SPRINT "<120" is not casual-office "<120".

## Context

Major driver of the lower targets in src-acc-aha-2017-htn. Landmark
