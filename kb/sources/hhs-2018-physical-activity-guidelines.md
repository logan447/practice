# HHS Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans (2018, 2nd ed.)

> **Worked example, seeded from Claude's training knowledge —
> `unverified` until checked against health.gov.**

## What it says

- Adults: 150–300 min/week moderate-intensity, or 75–150 min/week
  vigorous aerobic activity (or equivalent combination), **plus**
  muscle-strengthening ≥2 days/week.
- Some activity is better than none; benefits accrue below the
  threshold. The 2nd edition removed the ≥10-minute-bout requirement.
- "Move more, sit less" — sedentary time is an independent target.
- Additional benefit above 300 min/week; older adults add balance
  training and multicomponent activity.

## Population & scope

General population guidance (US); chronic-condition and older-adult
chapters not yet captured.

## Caveats & exceptions

- Counseling framing, not a disease-specific dosing document — for
  condition-specific effect sizes cite the condition's own sources
  (e.g., BP effects in src-acc-aha-2017-htn).
