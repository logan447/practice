# Project instructions

This is a preparation project for Direct Lifestyle Medicine: a solo,
direct-pay, membership-based adult practice combining prevention and
lifestyle medicine with medical treatment and care coordination.
Standard membership $99/month, with sliding-scale and pro bono
memberships (D-039, 2026-11-12). Start with `README.md` and
`docs/charter/working-model.md`.

The repositioning is implemented in `site/`, `print/brochure/` and
`docs/outreach/`. `docs/acquisition/`, `docs/agreements/` and most of
`docs/charter/` still carry the earlier "direct primary care,
$100/month" model and have NOT been reconciled. Check the site before
trusting an older document on positioning or price.

No print piece may state how Medicare or Medicaid work with a
membership until counsel settles it (D-039 guardrail 4b).

## Writing standard

`docs/style/README.md` (House Style) is the binding writing standard
for ALL patient-facing, public-facing, marketing, outreach, website,
and practice materials. Read it before drafting or editing any such
material. Its checklist (§9) gates every draft.

Non-negotiables it encodes: ~5th-grade plain language, no em dashes in
new copy, no marketing voice, exact credential language, no claims that
outrun evidence (R-13), never disparage other physicians or the
system, one CTA ("Book a free conversation"), canonical phrases
verbatim, no emojis.

## Repo rules

- Label every claim: facts sourced, estimates derived, assumptions
  owned. No invented numbers; unset inputs stay UNSET.
- Perishable figures carry `REFRESH-AT-RUNWAY`. Licensed-advisor
  questions carry `NEEDS-COUNSEL` / `NEEDS-CPA` / `NEEDS-BROKER`.
- Documents show the current model; superseded thinking moves to
  `docs/archive/` (D-025).
- Decisions get logged in `docs/charter/decision-log.md` with reasoning
  and revisit triggers.
- The website sketches (`docs/website/sketches/`) are approved copy:
  change only with owner approval, and keep print/site copy from
  forking (duplication rule).
