# Phased Acquisition Plan

*2026-08-21. What is actually being done, week by week, at each stage of
growth. Phases are gated by patient count and roadmap era, not by
calendar dates (Era-1 pacing rules apply — no deadlines, R-19).
Milestone months below are the researched ramp benchmarks (unit
economics §4) for reference, not commitments.*

**The organizing asymmetry (README §2, amendment 5):** launch is 12–24
months out. Era 1 builds **compounding assets**; Era 2 activates
**perishable activities**. Doing local-activation work now would decay
(R-16); skipping asset work now would waste the only long runway this
practice will ever have.

## Phase 0 — Era 1, pre-move (now): build the assets

## Phase 1 — Era 2 → first 10 patients (ramp months ~0–2)

**Context:** licensure/entity/EHR-billing runway items are landing
(roadmap Era 2); acquisition activates the moment enrollment is legally
and operationally possible. Break-even is ~19 patients — Phase 1+2 are
the survival phases, funded by moonlighting (R-03 mitigations).

**Where the first 10 realistically come from:** the announcement (B10)
+ the founding list (C1) + first referral-source relationships (B1–B3)
+ the ads test (A12) catching Tier 2/3 searchers. *Not* SEO (too
early), *not* reviews (none exist).

**Launch-week actions:**
- The **announcement**: personal notes (not a blast) to the
  professional network + the founding list gets the "doors open" issue.
- **GBP verified** + directory copy pack pasted everywhere (A4–A6,
  DPC Frontier mapper first); NAP consistency checked once.
- **Ads test live** (A12): exact-match Tier 2/3, defined budget,
  3-month window, cost-per-consult on the scorecard.
- **Outreach sprint begins** (B1–B3): target ~2 source visits/week —
  ranked list of ~25 sources built during runway from the Austin scan
  (Track F).

**Weekly rhythm (~6–8 h/wk on acquisition — deliberately front-loaded
while the panel is small; clinical load is light):**

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Tue + Thu mornings | one referral-source visit each (~1.5 h w/ travel) |
| Weekly | ~2 h: essay progress or letter; 15-min ledger sweep (every prospect has a next action) |
| Per consult | the follow-up rhythm (C3) — 24h recap, 1-wk check |
| Monthly | letter · scorecard (30 min) · one community talk if booked |
| From patient one | referral posture + review ask at natural moments (C5/C6); utilization tracked (Q-20) |

## Phase 2 — 10 → 25 patients (ramp months ~2–4)

**Emphasis shifts:** from *announcing* to *compounding relationships*.
The scorecard has its first real column of data — the quarterly
re-weighting (measurement §6) starts meaning something.

- Outreach continues at ~2 visits/wk, now including **second touches**:
  report-backs and thank-yous to sources whose referrals came through —
  the habit-forming step (funnel.md §5).
- **First employer conversations open** (B11) *if* Track F's employer
  exploration found warm paths — otherwise deferred to Phase 3. Broker
  compensation question must be through counsel first (Q-23).
- Ads test hits its 3-month call: kill, keep, or resize on
  cost-per-enrollee.
- Talks: ~1/month standing (B9); press pitch (A14) once there are
  patients and a true story to tell (R-13 pass on every quote).
- Review base: first Google reviews accruing via compliant asks.
- Content cadence: 1 essay + 1 letter per month, sustained.
- **First churn events will happen here.** Log reasons; do not
  over-steer on n=2.

**Milestone check (R-03):** researched benchmark says ~19–25 patients ≈
months 2–3 at 8–12 net adds/mo. If actual net adds run at <50% of plan
for two consecutive months, run the diagnostic *before* adding
channels: is the funnel starving at discovery (few consults), at fit
(consults but wrong people — check channel mix), or at conversion
(fit-consults not enrolling — check the friction map, funnel.md §3.1)?

## Phase 3 — 25 → 50 patients (ramp months ~4–7)

- **Engine balance check (R-26):** by 50 the mix is destiny — the
  scorecard's early-utilization flags decide which channels get the
  next quarter's hours. Employer lane (lighter ballast) should be
  actively in play by now: target one pilot group.
- SEO begins paying: Tier 3 (home visits) first, Tier 2 next — watch
  GSC; adjust essays toward what's almost-ranking.
- Referral engine: prune to sources that actually refer; deepen those
  (quarterly touches); patient referrals begin appearing — treat each
  referring patient's experience as the asset it is.
- Outreach visits taper to ~1/wk; time shifts to serving the growing
  panel (this is by design: outreach hours convert into care hours as
  compounding channels take over).
- Decide the YouTube/short-form question (A10) honestly: only if
  sustainable.

## Phase 4 — 50 → 140: the mature system (target ~months 14–18)

**Steady state to design toward:** acquisition runs at ~28
replacements/yr (R-03) on ~3–5 h/wk:

| Cadence | The mature engine |
|---|---|
| Weekly | 15-min ledger sweep; consults + follow-ups as they come |
| Monthly | letter · 1 essay-or-refresh · scorecard · 1 talk or source touch |
| Quarterly | re-weighting · source-list touches · content calendar |
| Annually | full audit (measurement §6) · essay refresh pass · packet/directory copy re-verification |

- Channels at maturity: word of mouth + patient referrals (largest
  share, prior to verify) · organic search + GBP (steady stranger
  flow) · the referral engine (~10–15 active sources) · employer
  groups (ballast + block enrollment) · the letter (the warm pool that
  makes panel-reopening announcements instant).
- **Panel-full posture:** when the panel approaches ~140, the machine
  doesn't stop — it *converts*: panel-status page flips, the letter
  becomes the waitlist, referral sources get the honest "waitlist +
  I'll still triage-and-route your people" promise. A full practice
  with a warm waitlist is the R-03 endgame: churn replacement from a
  standing start of zero effort.

## Standing failure-modes watch

- **Asset work that never activates** (Era-1 essays polished forever —
  R-18): the cornerstone list is 12, then stop and ship.
- **Activity metrics masquerading as results** (visits made, packets
  left — the scorecard counts enrollments and hours honestly).
- **The gate eroding under ramp pressure** (R-26): the fit-verdict
  column makes gate discipline visible month by month.
- **One channel quietly eating the calendar:** the hours column exists
  so "I spent 14 hours on outreach for one consult" is a finding, not
  a feeling.
- **Marketing register drift under growth anxiety:** the standing rules
  (README §5) are the check — no urgency, no schemes, no claims beyond
  evidence, even in a slow month.
