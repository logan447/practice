# Pre-Launch Campaign — Arriving With the Machine Already Moving

*2026-08-21. The 6–12-month pre-launch execution plan, built on the
acquisition architecture (`README.md`, `funnel.md`, `channels.md`).
Expands `phased-plan.md` Phase 0 into a full campaign. Timeline uses
**T-months before opening day** (T-9 as the working center of the 6–12
window); stages gate on real dependencies, not dates (Era-1 pacing,
R-19). Research is dated; planning numbers are labeled priors.*

**The objective, stated as the launch-day condition:** the website is
live and found; essays rank or are beginning to; the letter ships
monthly to a real audience; a founding list of Austin adults is waiting;
referral sources know the physician's name and have packets; the
funnel is instrumented — so opening day is the day an already-running
system *starts converting*, not the day marketing starts.

---

## 1. The pre-launch funnel, pressure-tested

The proposed structure (Discovery → Website → Newsletter/Waitlist →
Pre-Launch Relationship → Consultation when appropriate → Enrollment at
launch) is directionally right. Four amendments:

1. **The conversion event changes.** Pre-launch, the funnel's bottom is
   not a consultation — it is **joining the founding list**. Everything
   upstream (search, essays, talks, outreach) points there. The
   consultation stage doesn't disappear; it *compresses into the launch
   window* (§2's ladder, rung 5): once Texas licensure is in hand and
   opening is weeks away, founding conversations begin — so opening day
   has a queue of mutual-fit people ready to enroll, not a queue of
   strangers ready to be screened.
2. **Two audiences, two lists** (§4): the letter serves anyone who
   values the writing, anywhere; the founding list is Austin-specific
   expressed interest in *the practice*. Different intent, different
   promises, different cadence. They cross-pollinate but never merge.
3. **Referral sources run a parallel pre-launch funnel** with a
   different conversion event: not "send me patients" (impossible and
   presumptuous now) but **"knows me, wants to stay in touch, expects
   the opening"** — activation happens at launch (§6).
4. **The relationship stage needs designed touchpoints, not vibes.**
   "Pre-launch relationship" = the quarterly founding-list update, the
   monthly letter, and 2–4 open-house Q&A calls (§4.4) — scheduled,
   bounded, and honest about status. Nothing else, so nothing goes
   stale or over-promises.

```
PRE-LAUNCH:
discover (search, essays, talks, outreach, network)
   → website ("opening [season], Austin — not yet accepting patients")
   → letter (anyone) ─┐
   → FOUNDING LIST (Austin; the conversion event)
        → designed touchpoints: quarterly update · open-house Q&A · monthly letter
LAUNCH WINDOW (license in hand, ~T-4 wks):
        → founding conversations (fit gate, D-016) → enrollment queue
OPENING DAY:
        → enrollments execute (agreement + card + first visits) → the standing funnel takes over
```

## 2. The legal/ethical ladder — what happens when

The controlling facts: currently Virginia-licensed; not yet
Texas-licensed; no Texas location, entity, or practice. The ladder
sorts every pre-launch activity by what it legally *is*. Rungs 1–3 are
open now; rung 4 opens with the Texas license; rungs 5–7 open at
launch. (`compliance.md` §9 holds the flags; counsel items → Q-23.)

| Rung | Activity | Status | Design rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Marketing a future practice** | ✅ Now | Truthful and status-clear, always: "opening in Austin, [season] — not yet accepting patients." Never "Austin physician," never present-tense operating language, no bookable care. The **status line** appears on every surface: site footer, essay bylines, letter footer, GBP (when created), packets, talk intros. Credential guardrails apply (VA-licensed physician; TX license *pending* only once actually applied for) |
| 2 | **Collecting contact information** | ✅ Now | Name/email/ZIP-level location — never health information (§4.3). A marketing list, not a patient record; privacy promise stated at signup |
| 3 | **General health education & informational conversations** | ✅ Now | Essays, letters, talks, Q&A calls, one-on-one "how the practice will work" conversations — education about health topics and about the *model* is not the practice of medicine. Every piece carries the education-not-advice disclaimer. **The redirect discipline (the line that matters):** when anyone asks about *their* situation — "should I stop my statin?" — the answer is a warm, scripted redirect: *"That's exactly the kind of question we'd work through properly once the practice opens — for now, please take it to your current doctor."* No individualized assessment, no triage, no records kept on anyone's health. This is where unlicensed-practice risk lives; the script is the guardrail |
| 4 | **Founding conversations** (individual fit/intro conversations) | ⏳ TX license in hand, ~T-4 weeks | The D-016 conversation's *fit and logistics* half: their goals in general terms, the model, the price, the boundaries, mutual fit, what enrollment will look like. Clinical depth (records review, medical untangling) stays out — it belongs to the enrolled relationship anyway. No advice, no orders, no charge. `NEEDS-COUNSEL` confirmation of this boundary (Q-23) |
| 5 | **Clinical consultation / discussing someone's health substantively** | 🚫 Launch | Requires the open practice: TX license + entity + Terms + malpractice in force |
| 6 | **Physician-patient relationship + enrollment + payment** | 🚫 Launch | Agreement + card-on-file + first visit — the D-016 exit criterion. **No deposits, reservation fees, or paid waitlist ever** (prepayment for future care = DTPA/insurance-adjacent exposure + off-brand; the founding list is free by design). Medicare-age enrollees additionally wait on the opt-out sequence (Q-21 — see §7 timeline note) |
| 7 | **Care** | 🚫 Launch | The north-star workflow (roadmap Era 2 exit) |

Standing note: Virginia telehealth to established Virginia patients
(moonlighting) is untouched by any of this. The two worlds never mix:
no Texas prospect receives medical advice through any pre-launch
channel.

## 3. The critical path nobody thinks of as marketing

Two dependencies gate the whole campaign and must move first:

1. **The Texas license application.** TMB is mandated to average ~51
   days once an application is complete, but fingerprints, transcripts,
   and verification realistically put submission-to-issuance at **3–5.5
   months** (researched 2026-08-21: tmb.texas.gov; licensing-service
   estimates; `REFRESH-AT-RUNWAY`). Rung 4 — founding conversations,
   the step that turns the list into an enrollment queue — waits on it.
   **Recommendation: submit by T-6 at the latest; T-8 if the 6-month
   window is chosen.** This is a marketing-critical-path item as much
   as a regulatory one (feeds roadmap Era 2 item 1: "when settling is
   decided" should mean "the moment the 6–12-month clock starts").
2. **Publishing the website.** The prototype is currently gated on the
   TMB-advertising/counsel pass (design doc, R-13). A pre-launch
   campaign needs the site *live at ~T-9 to T-8*, which pulls that
   counsel pass forward from "runway" to "now-ish" — scoped to the
   site + status-line wording + the packet + directory copy in one
   sitting (Q-23 item 7). Budgeted counsel hours, spent early, buying
   9 months of compounding. Domain purchase: immediately, regardless.

## 4. The founding list (waitlist) — design

### 4.1 Separate from the letter — yes

| | **The letter** (newsletter) | **The founding list** (waitlist) |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Anyone who values the writing, anywhere | Austin-area adults interested in *joining the practice* |
| Intent signal | "Teach me" | "I may want to be your patient" |
| Cadence | Monthly | Quarterly update + the opening sequence |
| Content | Health education + light practice news | Practice progress: licensure milestones, location, opening window, how enrollment will work |
| Funnel role | MOF trust, indefinite | BOF queue, terminal at launch |
| At launch | Continues forever | Converts: first-access booking, then dissolves into patients/letter/closed |

One person can be on both (checkbox each way); the lists live as two
segments and are never merged with each other's promises. A letter
reader in Ohio should never get "book your founding conversation"; a
founding-list member should never need the essays to hear the opening
news.

### 4.2 The offer — concrete, so nobody signs up for silence

Joining the founding list means, verbatim promises:

1. **You'll hear from me roughly quarterly** with real progress —
   licensure, location, opening window — and I'll tell you when plans
   change. *(The anti-silence promise: cadence is stated at signup and
   kept. A quarterly update with actual milestones beats monthly
   filler.)*
2. **You get first access:** founding-list members can book
   introductory conversations ~2 weeks before booking opens publicly,
   and the first enrollment slots are yours. *(First access — not a
   guaranteed place: the panel-mix gate (D-016) still applies and is
   said plainly: "joining the list doesn't commit you to anything, and
   the practice enrolls people it can genuinely serve well.")*
3. **Invitations to open-house calls** (§4.4) to meet me and ask
   anything about the model.
4. **Optional:** the monthly letter (checkbox).
5. **Never:** payment, commitment, spam, or a sold/shared list. Leave
   anytime, one click.

No founding-member discount (price integrity, D-029/X-10). The perk is
access and being known by name — which at this practice is the product.

### 4.3 Data collected — the whole form

Name · email · ZIP (service-area planning) · "how did you hear about
the practice?" (attribution from day one) · number of interested adults
in household · *(optional)* "Are you enrolled in Medicare?" — asked as
logistics, with its reason printed: the practice serves
Medicare-enrolled members through a specific legal arrangement whose
paperwork takes sequencing (D-033/Q-21); a rough count changes the
opt-out calendar. **Deliberately absent: any health question, any
free-text box** (free text invites medical disclosures; the form says
so: "please don't include health information — that conversation comes
later, properly"). The list lives in the newsletter tool as its own
segment or a separate audience — not in a spreadsheet emailed around;
export-capable (R-15 spirit).

### 4.4 Designed touchpoints (the "pre-launch relationship" made concrete)

- **Quarterly founding update** (written): milestone-honest, short,
  one useful thing included.
- **Open-house Q&A calls, 2–4 total across the window** (~T-6, ~T-3,
  ~T-1): a group video call — 15 minutes of "here's the practice and
  where it stands," then open questions about the *model*. Scales
  relationship-building without 1:1 clinical creep; the rung-3 redirect
  script applies live ("great question about your situation — that's a
  founding-conversation topic once we're open"). Recorded intro
  segment doubles as site/YouTube content.
- **The opening sequence** (from ~T-4 weeks, license in hand):
  (a) "We have an opening date" note; (b) founding-conversation booking
  opens to the list; (c) opening day: enrollment opens; (d) ~T+2 weeks:
  booking opens publicly and the standing funnel takes over.

## 5. Channel-by-channel pre-launch map

Format per channel: **Start now → Waits → Asset built → At launch →
How it produces patients.**

### 5.1 SEO / organic search
- **Now:** buy domain; publish the site with status line; publish the
  Austin-relevant pages honestly framed ("opening [season]"): the
  Tier-2 anchor ("Direct primary care in Austin: an honest guide"),
  the Tier-3 anchor (home visits), pricing, the panel-status page
  (status: "opening — join the founding list"). Search Console from
  day one; watch the Tier 2/3 query watchlist monthly.
- **Waits:** map-pack presence (GBP, §5.5); "accepting new patients"
  page flip; local reviews.
- **Asset:** domain age + indexed, aging, internally-linked pages —
  the 6–12-month clock the user correctly identified only starts when
  pages exist. Starting at T-9 vs. T-0 is the difference between
  ranking in launch quarter and ranking in month 9 of operation.
- **At launch:** page-1 presence for the Tier-3 wedge and credible
  movement on Tier-2 *(realistic aim, not a promise — new domains vary
  widely)*.
- **Patients:** high-intent strangers land → book page → standing funnel.

### 5.2 Essays / educational content
- **Now:** the full engine — §8's one-pipeline system. All 12
  cornerstones by ~T-2, sequenced SEO-first (Austin guide and
  home-visits essay earliest; they need the most aging).
- **Waits:** nothing. This channel runs at 100% pre-launch.
- **Asset:** the library — the single largest compounding pre-launch
  investment.
- **At launch:** 12 published, oldest 8+ months, several ranking
  long-tail; each with letter-signup and founding-list block for
  Austin-flagged pages.
- **Patients:** search → trust → list → launch conversion; plus every
  essay is outreach ammunition (a useful thing to send a source).

### 5.3 The letter
- **Now:** issue 1 this month. Monthly, forever. Public register:
  "physician preparing a practice; learning in public."
- **Waits:** nothing.
- **Asset:** the audience + the writing habit + deliverability history.
- **At launch:** 200–400 subscribers *(planning band, §7)*; the launch
  announcement lands on a warm list.
- **Patients:** slow-burn MOF → some subscribers are/know Austinites;
  the "forward this" line works geography-free.

### 5.4 The founding list
- **Now:** live on the site from publish day (§4). Every channel points
  here.
- **Waits:** conversion (rungs 4–6).
- **Asset:** the enrollment queue.
- **At launch:** 75–150 households *(planning band, §7)* → founding
  conversations → opening-week enrollments.
- **Patients:** directly — this *is* the pipeline.

### 5.5 Google Business Profile / local search
- **Now:** nothing possible (no address) except the copy pack.
- **From address-in-hand (~T-3 to T-1):** create the profile with a
  **future opening date** — GBP supports opening dates up to a year
  out, and verified profiles surface ~90 days pre-opening (researched
  2026-08-21: Google Business Profile Help; `REFRESH-AT-RUNWAY` —
  verify service-area-business rules for the home-visit model at
  setup). Photos, category, the status-honest description.
- **Asset:** a verified, aging profile that exits "recently opened"
  earlier.
- **At launch:** live profile day one; first reviews begin (compliant
  asks, from real patients only).
- **Patients:** map-pack for Tier-1/2 queries — the largest local
  stranger channel at maturity.

### 5.6 Directories
- **Now:** copy pack finalized; **DPC Frontier mapper** checked for
  "opening soon" listing policy *(unverified — their add-practice flow
  will say; if allowed, list early with the status line)*.
- **From address:** all citations (Apple/Bing, chamber, DPC Alliance,
  health directories claim-only) in one sitting from the pack.
- **Asset:** the pack + early DPC-community visibility.
- **At launch:** NAP-consistent presence everywhere that matters.
- **Patients:** small steady high-intent trickle + local-SEO substrate.

### 5.7 Physician referrals — incl. the overlooked channel: Austin's DPC community
- **Now (remote):** map the sources: every Austin DPC practice
  (the mapper is the census), the specialist/therapist/pharmacist list
  from the Track F Austin scan. Begin **collegial introductions** —
  email/call: *"I'm a physician preparing to open a DPC practice in
  Austin next year; I admire what you've built; could I ask you 20
  minutes about the market?"* DPC physicians are famously collegial,
  full practices maintain waitlists they'd rather route somewhere
  good, and a peer asking to learn is welcome where a marketer isn't.
  These calls are simultaneously Track F validation research.
- **Trips (§6):** convert the best remote contacts to in-person.
- **Waits:** any referral ask (nothing to refer *to*), packets in
  hands (deliver near launch so they don't stale on a shelf).
- **Asset:** the relationship ledger at stage "warm" (§6.2).
- **At launch:** 20–30 introduced, 8–12 met in person, 3–5 who've said
  "tell me when you're open" unprompted *(planning bands, §7)*.
- **Patients:** activation notes at T-0 with packets; the Engine B
  habit loop (`funnel.md` §5) starts turning.

### 5.8 Community relationships (churches, orgs, senior-serving, gyms)
- **Now (remote):** identify + light contact only where a real hook
  exists (senior-serving orgs overlap the physician's existing
  professional world — the warmest community lane; use it).
- **Trips:** one or two community meetings per trip; a talk delivered
  *on a trip* is the strongest version ("I'm moving here to do this").
- **Waits:** deep local embedding — it genuinely requires living there;
  don't fake it, budget it for post-move.
- **Asset:** a short list of orgs that know the name + one reusable
  talk, field-tested.
- **At launch:** 3–5 venues that would host a talk in the first 90
  days.
- **Patients:** talks → list signups → conversations (the B9 path).

### 5.9 Employers / brokers
- **Now:** research-only (Track F's employer exploration): what small
  Austin employers buy, who the DPC-friendly brokers are. Maybe 2–3
  informational conversations on trips.
- **Waits:** all selling (no proof, no product, no broker structure —
  Q-23 counsel gate).
- **Asset:** the target list + market understanding + the one-pager
  draft.
- **At launch:** dormant by design; activates at ~25 patients
  (`phased-plan.md` Phase 2–3).
- **Patients:** later — the ballast lane (README §4).

### 5.10 Direct outreach (calls, mail, trips)
- **Now:** the systematic remote campaign (§6): ~2 introductions/week,
  tracked in the ledger.
- **Waits:** mass mail (packets go out near launch, not into the
  void 9 months early); any "send me patients."
- **Asset:** the relationship ledger — dozens of named, staged,
  next-touch-dated contacts.
- **At launch:** the activation wave (§6.3) lands on warm ground.
- **Patients:** via Engine B from week one instead of month six.

### 5.11 Social / video
- **Now:** decide honestly (the A10 sustainability rule). If yes:
  the open-house intro recordings and 2–3 essay-voiced videos are the
  cheap on-ramp — no channel-building treadmill. LinkedIn: the
  physician's existing professional presence carries launch-journey
  posts at near-zero cost to the schedule; that's the one social
  channel with a standing audience.
- **Waits:** everything else; short-form as a program is a post-launch
  question.
- **Asset:** a handful of videos where prospects can *meet the
  physician's manner* — the highest-trust asset a stranger can find.
- **Patients:** MOF trust accelerant; minor TOF via YouTube search.

### 5.12 Paid
- **Now:** nothing. Paid traffic to a practice that can't enroll
  buys list-signups at cash cost while organic does it free — and the
  founding list should be built on trust channels *(position, not
  dogma: revisit at ~T-2 only if the list is badly under target — a
  small "opening soon" search test on Tier-2/3 terms is the one
  defensible shape)*.
- **At launch:** the A12 test as designed (`channels.md`).

### 5.13 Word of mouth / the existing network
- **Now:** the quiet version — colleagues, mentors, the senior-care
  professional world, friends-of-friends in Texas hear "I'm building
  this, opening next year" in normal conversation + a personal note
  when the site goes live ("no ask — just wanted you to know what I'm
  building; the letter's here if you're curious").
- **Waits:** the launch announcement proper (B10) — one designed,
  personal wave at T-0, not dribbled out early.
- **Asset:** a warm network that already knows, so the launch note is
  a confirmation, not news.
- **Patients:** referrals from people who trust the physician —
  historically every solo practice's true first channel.

### 5.14 Press / podcasts *(added channel)*
- **Now:** guest on **DPC-community podcasts** (a real niche with
  steady guest demand — *unverified specifics; scan at T-6*): the
  "physician-researcher building a $100 flat-rate practice in public"
  story is unusual enough to book. National audience, but it builds
  the searchable footprint and DPC-community standing that Austin
  colleagues will find.
- **Waits:** Austin local press until launch (the story needs a real
  practice; "opens today, still makes house calls" is the hook).
- **Asset:** authority footprint + backlinks (the legitimate kind).
- **Patients:** indirect — authority, SEO, community referrals.

## 6. The pre-launch outreach system (Engine B, remote edition)

### 6.1 The campaign shape
~2 introductions/week × ~36 weeks ≈ 70 attempted, expecting roughly
half to become real conversations *(prior)*. Sequenced by
receptivity-to-remote-contact: Austin DPC physicians → senior-care
orgs (existing world) → therapists/pharmacists (harder cold; better on
trips) → community orgs (mostly on trips) → specialists (mostly at
launch, when there's something concrete to coordinate around).

### 6.2 Tracked, not vibed — relationship stages in the source ledger
`identified → contacted → conversed → met in person → warm (agreed to
stay connected) → activated (has packet, knows opening date) → first
referral`. Every row: next-touch date. Cadence promise mirrors the
founding list: quarterly, substantive, short. **The anti-sales-campaign
rule:** every touch either gives something (a useful essay, a market
insight from the research, a reverse referral once operating) or asks
a genuine question. Nothing is a "just checking in."

### 6.3 Austin trips — 2–3, purpose-built
- **Trip 1 (~T-6):** validation + relationship seeding. 8–12 meetings
  booked in advance (the remote-contact conversion event), 1 community
  talk if bookable, neighborhoods/service-area recon, Track F price
  validation live.
- **Trip 2 (~T-2 to T-3):** consolidation. Second meetings with the
  warm; location/space decisions (X-05 groundwork); GBP address
  question resolved; a talk; possibly local counsel/CPA meetings
  (stacking runway errands onto marketing trips).
- **Trip 3 = the move.**
- **The activation wave (T-0):** every warm contact gets the personal
  opening note + packet delivered/mailed; the ask finally happens:
  "I'm open. Here's exactly what happens when you send someone."

## 7. Launch position — targets derived, then the backward timeline

### 7.1 What launch needs (derivation from unit economics)
Break-even ~19 patients; researched ramp 8–12 net adds/month reaching
~19–25 around month 2–3 (unit economics §4). **The pre-launch campaign's
job, quantified: put the practice at or past break-even inside the
first 90 days — 1–2 months ahead of the researched cold-start pace.**

Working the founding list backward *(all conversion figures are
planning priors — no researched DPC waitlist-conversion data found;
replace with ledger actuals from the opening sequence)*: list of N →
~60–70% still engaged after months of waiting (decay is real; the
quarterly cadence is its mitigation) → ~50–60% of those book a founding
conversation in the launch quarter → ~85% complete → ~60–75% mutual-fit
(self-selected population) → ~60–75% of fit enroll. Net: **roughly
15–25% of the founding list becomes enrolled patients in the launch
quarter.**

## 8. The content engine for a physician with limited time

One unit of work, five outputs, ~5–6 h/month total:

```
one real question (from validation calls, moonlighting patterns, the list's questions)
   → the essay (3–4 h, cornerstone quality, evergreen)
      → the letter issue (+45 min: intro paragraph + the essay + one small thing)
      → the SEO page (it IS one — query-titled, internally linked, 0 extra hours)
      → outreach ammunition (sent to 2–3 relevant sources as the quarter's give)
      → (optional, if A10 = yes) a talked-through video version (+1–2 h, batch 2–3 per sitting)
```

Rules that keep it sustainable: the monthly unit is **one essay + one
letter, full stop** — a backlog month ships a shorter letter without an
essay rather than breaking cadence; essays are finished, not perfected
(R-18); the 12-cornerstone list is the scope — new ideas go to a
someday-list until the library is done; writing time is calendared as
one weekly block, not found.

## 9. Pre-launch failure modes

- **Status drift:** a directory listing or forwarded packet that reads
  as "practicing in Austin now." Mitigation: the status line is on
  *every* artifact, and every artifact is in the ad archive
  (`compliance.md` §1) with a T-0 sweep to flip them all.
- **The list goes cold:** the quarterly promise slips once and trust
  spends fast. The cadence is calendared like clinic.
- **Clinical creep:** rung-3 conversations sliding into rung-5
  territory because being helpful is the physician's instinct. The
  redirect script, practiced, plus no health data anywhere.
- **License timing slips** and the T-1 conversation window collapses
  into post-move chaos. Mitigation: submit ≤T-6, track TMB status
  monthly, keep a fallback ("conversations start two weeks late"
  beats improvising rung boundaries).
- **Asset polishing replaces shipping** (R-18): the scoreboard (§7.2)
  is the antidote — it counts published, sent, contacted, booked.
- **Over-promising the opening date:** announce a *season* until the
  license is in hand; a slipped specific date costs more trust than a
  vague-but-kept window.
