## Design questions the re-baseline created (answer during preparation)

### Q-19 — The boundary design: what sits outside $100/month? `YOU` + me
The flat rate's patient-friendly guardrails, designed once and stated up
front in the Practice Terms: the carve-out list (e.g., extensive
medico-legal paperwork and forms, convenience home visits beyond clinical
indication, sustained near-daily-contact phases, travel beyond a radius),
whether any carry separate published charges (a 🟩 design choice for cash
patients per the payment research), and the language that keeps
boundaries from ever arriving as surprises. **This is now the most
important patient-facing design artifact after the price itself** —
it is what protects both the panel math (unit economics §3) and the
promise of simplicity.

### Q-20 — Panel-mix instrumentation `YOU` + me
The flat rate stands or falls on mix (heavy users ≈ 1 in 8–10). Needs: a
simple per-patient utilization measure the EHR/tooling must support
(feeds X-03 requirements), the onboarding capacity-check questions
(D-016), and the renewal-conversation script for sustained outliers.
Descends from the old R-08 estimate-vs-actual discipline.

### Q-07 — Availability policy and triage taxonomy, written for a 140-patient panel `YOU` + me
Unchanged in content (D-017), upgraded in load: emergency → ED; urgent →
urgent care; message-me; can-wait — now as portal-native patient
education that works at panel scale, plus the away-period protocol.

### Q-09 — Operationalize the value dashboard `YOU` + `RESEARCH`
D-023 trends (vitals, labs, symptoms, function, medication burden,
well-being, encounters, spend) — instrument choices, cadence, and what
the EHR must support (feeds X-03).

### Q-16 — Home-visit operations `YOU` + `RESEARCH`
Kit by procedural scope, safety protocol, clustering/geography rules,
field documentation, and the clinical-indication criteria that keep home
visits from becoming an on-demand amenity (~40% of a patient's annual
hours budget per visit).

### Q-02 — Payer knowledge (research track) `YOU`

## Verification questions (design now, verify at runway)

### Q-17 — Minimum availability/continuity structure `RESEARCH` → `COUNSEL` + `BROKER`
Does bounded-availability-without-coverage satisfy carriers,
standard-of-care/abandonment norms, and TMB expectations — for a
~140-patient panel with ~13 weeks/year of aware-not-absent time? Brief
during preparation; verify with Texas counsel + carrier at runway; X-06
reopens only if a higher floor is found.

### Q-21 — Medicare-age patients under a flat direct-pay membership `COUNSEL`
The healthy-aging mission meets the researched constraints: mandatory
claims for covered services, no opt-out while moonlighting, membership
fees restricted to genuinely non-covered services for enrolled
beneficiaries. What, exactly, can be offered to a 68-year-old at launch —
and is it worth offering before opt-out is possible? Top of the counsel
agenda.

### Q-15 — Compliance pass on the simple model `COUNSEL`
Much smaller than before: the Practice Terms against the Texas DPC
statute's conjunctive definition (acute + chronic + preventive +
continuity); GFE mechanics for a published flat price; refund/
cancellation terms (DTPA); discounted-slot criteria (X-10, R-10);
marketing claims (R-13).

### Q-18 — Compensation and tax structure `CPA`
The after-tax goal spans a ~$20k pre-tax band on filing status, QBI,
entity structure (W-2 not assumed), SE tax, retirement strategy, and the
"is retirement saving inside or on top of $100k" definition. Also:
monthly-billing revenue accounting. Engage before runway pricing is
final.
