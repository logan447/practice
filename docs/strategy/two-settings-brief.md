# Two Care Settings Brief — FCHC Employment + the PLLC

*Research brief 2026-08-22 (background agent, primary sources cited).
Question: is the "different care settings" concept sound — Medicare
prospects cared for at FCHC where he's employed, non-Medicare adults
in the private membership practice — and what boundaries does it
need? Labels: VERIFIED / LIKELY / OWNER-FACT / NEEDS-COUNSEL.*

## Verdict

**Legally sound with guardrails; operationally thinner than it
feels.** Dual practice is normal. Directing a Medicare prospect to
"see me at FCHC" is not a Stark referral (personally-performed
services are excluded from "referral," 42 CFR 411.351, and ordinary
E/M is not a designated health service); the bona fide employment
exception (411.357(c)) and AKS employee safe harbor (1001.952(i))
cover the salary relationship; truthful information to a beneficiary
is not remuneration under the inducement CMP. But FCHC's public pages
suggest the door may be narrow (uninsured program <200% FPL,
Fredericksburg-area counties; one directory snapshot said FCHC was
not accepting new Medicare patients — possibly stale), and the
~50-mile drive means few NoVa/DC prospects convert. **Treat the FCHC
door as an honest answer for the occasional caller, not a patient
pathway, until FCHC confirms the owner-facts.**

## Key findings

1. **Dual practice lawful/normal.** `VERIFIED by absence` — the only
   binding constraint is the employment contract (`OWNER-FACT`).
2. **Stark:** E/M visits aren't DHS; self-performed care isn't a
   "referral"; residual DHS exposure (FCHC in-house lab/imaging) is
   covered by the employment exception if pay is FMV, not
   volume-of-referrals based. `OWNER-FACT`: any productivity comp →
   `NEEDS-COUNSEL`.
3. **AKS:** salary for treating patients, never for delivering them;
   employee safe harbor is broad. Confirm no per-referral bonus.
4. **Beneficiary inducement:** information transfers nothing of
   value; never sweeten (no waived fees, gifts, transport).
5. **Reverse direction is the radioactive one.** Never solicit FCHC
   patients (duty of loyalty, contract, charitable-mission optics,
   AKS optics). Even patient-initiated moves need a written policy:
   decline absent FCHC written consent. Medicare-covered FCHC
   patients couldn't join the membership anyway.
6. **Records:** FCHC owns its charts; never self-serve across the
   wall with his FCHC login. Cross-setting exchange only via HIM
   records request or patient authorization (45 CFR 164.506
   treatment disclosures are permitted, routed properly).
7. **Malpractice:** FCHC coverage covers FCHC work only. FCHC bills
   Medicare/Medicaid so it is NOT a §233(o) FTCA free clinic; if it's
   a §330 health center, FTCA deeming still wouldn't touch the PLLC.
   Own policy regardless.
8. **No commingling** of space, staff, supplies, time. Never see
   private members at/through FCHC.
9. **Marketing:** "I also practice at FCHC" is truthful under
   18VAC85-20-30 (note: 18VAC85-20-90 is repealed); bio-line use is
   fine, marketing use of FCHC's name needs written permission
   (`OWNER-FACT`). Medicare-caller script: state the rule plainly,
   name FCHC **plus at least one other option** (information, not
   steering).
10. **Ethics (AMA 11.2.2, 11.2.5):** retainer practices are
    ethical with fidelity, equal quality, clear terms; disclose the
    FCHC affiliation when mentioning it. The two-tier optic is
    favorable if honest: he adds charity-clinic hours most concierge
    physicians don't — while the never-recruit line holds.

## Guardrail checklist

1. Do not opt out of Medicare (breaks FCHC billing for his services).
2. Membership excludes Medicare/Medicaid beneficiaries; attestation +
   notify-on-enrollment clause.
3. Written bright line: never solicit FCHC patients; patient-initiated
   requests declined absent FCHC written consent.
4. FCHC comp = FMV salary; no referral/volume bonus.
5. Mentioning FCHC → disclose affiliation + name another option.
6. Zero commingling; separate EMR, phone, scheduling, records.
7. Records cross the wall only via HIM request or authorization.
8. Own malpractice for the PLLC; confirm FCHC coverage scope.
9. FCHC name in marketing only with written permission.
10. Enrollment notice: practices are separate; FCHC care unaffected.
11. Nothing of value ever offered to steer a beneficiary.

## Ready-to-ask FCHC questions (OWNER-FACT)

1. Does my employment agreement restrict outside practice; may I have
   written confirmation my own direct-pay practice in NoVa is fine?
2. Are you accepting new Medicare patients? Residency/service-area
   requirements for insured patients?
3. If a NoVa Medicare prospect asked to establish care with me here,
   is that welcome? Intake steps?
4. Do I control my schedule/paneling here?
5. Is any of my comp tied to volume, panel size, or referrals?
6. What malpractice covers my work here (carrier vs FTCA)? Certificate?
7. May my private site say I also practice at FCHC — approved wording?
8. If an FCHC patient asks to join my private practice, what do you
   want me to do?
9. Is FCHC an FQHC/§330 center, look-alike, or private charitable
   clinic?

## NEEDS-COUNSEL

Employment-contract review; membership Medicare-exclusion +
aging-in off-ramp; 411.357(c)/1001.952(i) fit if comp has
productivity component; cross-setting script check; PLLC malpractice
application disclosure of dual practice (`NEEDS-BROKER`).
