# Patient Archetypes & Example Care Agreements

This area makes the practice concrete: six representative patient archetypes,
each with a **patient-facing example proposal** (what a real person would see
before agreeing to care), the **money flow from the patient's perspective**,
and the **physician-side economics** — so every arrangement can be checked
against the two-sided test: *a good deal for the patient AND a sustainable
use of physician time.*

## Status and ground rules

- **These are design drafts, not legal documents.** Formal agreements sit
  underneath them later, written with Texas counsel (`NEEDS-COUNSEL`). No
  proposal here is used with a real patient before that review.
- **All prices are illustrative**, generated by the D-002 framework at a
  scenario rate (noted in each file). They exist to test whether arrangements
  *feel* fair and are *economically* coherent — not to set the price list.
- **Insurance-related content is conditional.** Anything involving billing
  insurance, superbills, or hybrid structures depends on the findings in
  `payment-landscape.md` and on counsel/payer verification (Q-15, X-04).
  Those sections say so explicitly.
- Vignettes are **fictional composites**, not real people.

## The archetypes

| | File | Who | Engagement shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| P | `archetype-P-prevention.md` | Healthy; wants prevention, lifestyle improvement, a physician who knows them | Ongoing, light |
| S | `archetype-S-stable-chronic.md` | 1–2 controlled chronic conditions | Ongoing, moderate |
| M | `archetype-M-active-management.md` | Active metabolic/chronic disease work, medication adjustment | Ongoing, intensive |
| C | `archetype-C-complex-diagnostic.md` | Multiple unresolved symptoms; needs real evaluation and follow-up | Front-loaded year, then step-down |
| U | `archetype-U-whole-life.md` | **Signature**: overwhelmed; health tangled with stress, work, money, relationships, sleep | Ongoing, intensive early |
| E | `archetype-E-defined-problem.md` | One defined problem to solve; no indefinite relationship wanted | Time-boxed episode |

Real patients blend archetypes; these are reference points the proposal
process (D-016) tailors from, not boxes patients are placed in.

## Companion documents

- `payment-landscape.md` — researched payment norms and the
  insurance-hybrid question: what is common practice, what is legally
  required, what is payer-dependent, what is our design choice. Dated;
  `REFRESH-AT-RUNWAY`.
- `pricing-conversation.md` — how pricing is introduced across the
  complimentary consultation so the proposal lands as "that seems fair,"
  never as a surprise.

## The shared proposal skeleton

Every example follows the same bones (the Q-05 template): what I heard →
what I believe I can help with → the plan (scope, initial evaluation,
cadence, settings, async care) → duration and checkpoints → what we will
track → what's included / not included → the economics → how payment works.
