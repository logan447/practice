# Archetype U — Whole-Life Untangler (Signature)

**Who:** Fictional composite — "Sarah," 49, everything at once: blood
pressure creeping up, prediabetes, 15 pounds in two years, sleeping five
broken hours, drinking more than she means to, a marriage strained by money
stress, a job that consumes her, and a growing fear that her health is
unraveling. She can't tell which problems are urgent, which are important,
and where to start. **This is the person the practice exists for** (D-014).

**Engagement shape:** ongoing; intensive untangling phase, then steady
working rhythm.

---

## The proposal Sarah would see

> ### Care Proposal — prepared for Sarah · [date]
>
> **What I heard.** It's not one thing — it's everything, and it feeds on
> itself: the stress wrecks the sleep, the sleep wrecks the eating and the
> drinking, the money worry sits under all of it, and your body is starting
> to keep score. Mostly: you're overwhelmed, and no one has ever looked at
> the whole picture at once.
>
> **What I believe I can help with.** Untangling it. Not everything at
> once, and not all by me — but sorted: what is medically urgent, what is
> important, what order to work in, what I treat, where a counselor or
> financial professional does what I shouldn't, and one realistic plan
> instead of eight competing anxieties.
>
> **Here is how I see your situation** *(the proposal names it plainly)*:
> - **Needs medical attention now:** blood pressure — confirmed and treated
>   if it holds; alcohol pattern — honestly assessed, addressed medically
>   if needed.
> - **Important, workable over months:** prediabetes and weight (one food
>   system, not a diet), sleep (fix the physiology and the schedule
>   pressure driving it).
> - **Underneath everything:** the stress load — work boundaries, the money
>   conversation with your husband. I will help you think clearly about
>   these as *health* problems, connect you to the right professionals, and
>   keep them on our shared agenda — I don't replace a therapist or a
>   financial advisor.
>
> **The plan.**
> - **Extended evaluation** (~2 hours, split across two sittings): full
>   medical evaluation plus the whole-life map above, ending with a
>   one-page plan with priorities in order.
> - **Untangling phase (~3 months):** visit every 2 weeks (~40 min,
>   telemedicine): work the priority list — treat what needs treating,
>   one behavior experiment at a time, referrals made and integrated.
> - **Working rhythm (months 4–12):** monthly, holding the plan steady as
>   life pushes back.
> - **Between visits:** messaging — because for this kind of work, the
>   moment something wobbles on a Tuesday matters; ~1–2 business-day
>   response.
>
> **What we'll track:** BP, glucose, weight, sleep, alcohol, and — just as
> seriously — stress, energy, mood, and your own rating of how manageable
> life feels. Plus what you've spent. **In a year, you and I should both be
> able to look at one page and see whether your life is actually better.**
>
> **Duration & checkpoints.** 12 months; checkpoints at 3 and 6 months —
> is the plan realistic, is the pace humane, is it working.
>
> **Included:** the extended evaluation, all visits, messaging, referrals
> and coordination (counseling, financial professional, specialists),
> the tracking above.
> **Not included:** therapy itself, financial advice itself (I help you
> think and connect you well — licensed professionals do their work);
> labs/medications/specialists (your insurance, as now); after-hours
> coverage.
>
> **The economics.** **~$4,700 for the 12-month engagement** — about
> $390/month. Roughly 18–19 hours of my time, weighted to the first three
> months, at the practice's standard rate with a complexity adjustment.
> What you're buying is not visits — it's the whole picture held by one
> competent person until it's manageable.

## Money flow — patient's perspective

**Scenario 1 · Private-pay (default):** $390/mo card on file; insurance
unchanged for labs, meds, therapy (often insurance-covered — part of the
referral design), specialists. Known total: ~$4,700/yr.

**Scenario 2 · Hybrid (researched 2026-08):** the *medical* spine (E/M
visits for HTN, prediabetes, alcohol counseling) is plausibly billable —
but the research turned the caution into a finding (`payment-landscape.md`
§1): the whole-life layer this archetype is *named for* — the extra time,
the whole-picture assessment and plan, the coordination — is precisely the
inventory the 2004 OIG Alert treats as embedded in covered services, and
what hold-harmless clauses protect. The compliant hybrid for Sarah exists
only with careful legal engineering, and it would constrain how the fee
describes the very thing she's buying. **Market note:** her ~$4,700/yr
arrangement sits inside the concierge fee band ($2,400–5,000) and well
below functional-medicine program pricing ($5–15k) — the private-pay
version is market-plausible on its own.
