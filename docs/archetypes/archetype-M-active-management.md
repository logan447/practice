# Archetype M — Active Metabolic / Chronic Disease Management

**Who:** Fictional composite — "James," 51, A1c 8.9% and climbing, BMI 34,
borderline blood pressure, statin he stopped taking, ready — actually ready
this time — to turn it around. Needs medication changes, tight feedback
loops, and a partner in the work, not a lecture and a follow-up in six
months.

**Engagement shape:** ongoing, intensive early. Front-loaded cadence that
relaxes as control improves.

---

## The proposal James would see

> ### Care Proposal — prepared for James · [date]
>
> **What I heard.** Your diabetes is not controlled and you know it. You've
> been told to "diet and exercise" for a decade without anyone actually
> working the problem with you. You're ready to do the work if someone
> designs it with you and adjusts fast when something isn't working.
>
> **What I believe I can help with.** Getting your A1c genuinely down over
> 6–12 months; medication changes done properly — including whether newer
> agents fit you; food, movement, and sleep changes sized to your real life;
> momentum that survives week six.
>
> **The plan.**
> - **Comprehensive evaluation** (~90 min, in person) to map the full
>   picture and set the plan and targets together.
> - **Phase 1 — first 3 months:** visit every 2–3 weeks (~30 min,
>   telemedicine): glucose data review, medication titration, one focused
>   lifestyle experiment at a time.
> - **Phase 2 — months 4–12:** monthly, stretching toward quarterly as
>   control arrives.
> - **Between visits:** your glucose numbers and questions by message —
>   I review and respond within 1–2 business days; sooner for concerning
>   values. Labs at 3, 6, and 12 months, reviewed together.
>
> **What we'll track:** A1c, glucose patterns, weight, BP, lipids,
> medications (count, doses, cost), energy, sleep, and how you feel —
> all visible to you as trends, next to what you've spent. **The goal is
> that in 12 months the graph itself tells you whether this was worth it.**
>
> **Duration & checkpoints.** 12-month initial engagement with a
> formal checkpoint at 3 and 6 months: are the numbers moving, is the
> effort sustainable, does the plan need re-scoping. Renewal at 12 months
> usually looks like a lighter arrangement — that's success.
>
> **Included:** all visits and the intensive early cadence, message-based
> titration support, lab interpretation, coordination (diabetes education,
> ophthalmology, etc.).
> **Not included:** labs, medications, devices (CGM etc.), specialists —
> through your insurance as now; after-hours coverage; ER/urgent care.
>
> **The economics.** **~$3,700 for the 12-month engagement** — about
> $310/month, front-loaded work, flat price. Roughly 16 hours of my time
> at the practice's standard rate with a modest complexity adjustment. If
> we succeed and year two is a lighter arrangement, the price drops to
> match — you are never paying an "intensive" price for maintenance care.

## Money flow — patient's perspective

**Scenario 1 · Private-pay (default):** $310/mo card on file for 12 months
(or quarterly/annual). Insurance keeps covering labs, medications, CGM,
specialists. Out-of-pocket to me: ~$3,700 for the year, known in advance.

**Scenario 2 · Hybrid (conditional):** frequent E/M visits for active
titration are plausibly billable covered services; copay burden (~10–14
visits × copay) partially offsets a reduced private fee. Same unresolved
legal core as archetype S; additionally, the *newer diabetes agents* often
require prior authorizations — which is real physician/admin time that
insurance participation adds (Q-02 admin-burden question, made concrete).

**Two-sided test:** James pays ~$10/day during the year he actually changes
his metabolic trajectory — against the cost trajectory of uncontrolled
diabetes, the value story is the strongest of any archetype and the
outcome is *measurable on his own portal*. Physician earns above target
rate for genuinely engaging work. **Passes strongly — this archetype is
likely the practice's best proof-of-value engine (D-023).**
