## Standing rules (bind every surface and all future copy)

1. **No emojis, anywhere, ever.** Icons are drawn line-icons or plain
   typographic marks.
2. **Copy filter:** clarity, thoughtfulness, directness, conciseness,
   maturity, professionalism, simplicity — every sentence.
3. **Credential accuracy:** internship (GW), never residency or
   board-certification implied; IBLM certification "in progress";
   self-description "physician / primary care physician," never
   "internist." (design.md rule 3)
4. **One primary CTA site-wide:** "Book a free conversation," with its
   de-risking subtext. Price appears on the homepage hero.
5. **No PHI on this site, ever** — booking collects name, email, phone,
   referral source only.
6. **Canonical phrases reused verbatim wherever they appear:** "away
   means slower — never unaware" · "when my panel is full, it is full" ·
   "that is the whole catch" · "if I'm not the right fit, I'll say so
   and point you toward someone good" · the emergency strip.
7. **No dark patterns:** no urgency, no countdowns, no exit popups, no
   hidden pricing.
