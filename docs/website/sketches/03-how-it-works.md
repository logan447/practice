narratable. Spine: "your first 90 days." Carries the D-017 boundary

### 2 · Your first 90 days (light band; vertical timeline, five stops)

**Before anything: a free conversation.** Thirty minutes by video. You
tell me what's going on; I tell you honestly whether I can help. If we
both feel good about it, you join — $100/month, cancel anytime. If not,
no hard feelings and no bill.

**Week 1–2: I do my homework, then we meet properly.** I request and
actually read your records before your first visit — you shouldn't have
to re-tell years of history from memory. Then we have your first full
visit: up to 90 minutes, everything on the table — history,
medications, concerns, goals. Longer or split into two sittings if your
situation needs it.

**Week 2–4: we fill in the picture.** Labs if we need them (I'll tell
you the cash price before anything is drawn, or we'll use your
insurance). An exam — at your home or at my clinic space — if your
situation calls for hands-on. Every result comes back explained, in
plain words.

**By about week 4: you have a plan.** A short written plan in your
portal — what we're working on, in what order, what I'm doing, what
you're doing, and when we'll check progress. Not a lecture. A plan we
both agreed to.

**Then: the rhythm that fits your situation.** Some patients see me
every two weeks while we work through something active. Some check in
monthly, or quarterly, or mostly message me between an annual deep
review. There's no required schedule and no per-visit charge — your
care fits your health, and around 90 days we look at how it's going.

### 3 · Where care happens (white; 3 cards `[icon]` markers, drawn icons at build — no emojis)

- `[icon: video]` **By video — most of the time.** Unhurried video
  visits from wherever you are. Most primary care — follow-ups,
  results, med changes, new concerns — works beautifully this way when
  there's enough time.
- `[icon: home]` **At your home — when it matters.** When an exam
  genuinely needs hands-on and home is the right place, I come to you.
  Scheduled ahead, within my [service area — LATER]. No extra charge —
  it's part of how I practice.
- `[icon: clinic]` **At my clinic space — when that's better.** For
  exams and procedures better done in an equipped room. Scheduled as
  needed.

> Where each visit happens is a clinical judgment we make together —
> video for most things, in person when your body needs to be in the
> room. I don't run a big office, and you're not paying for one.

### 4 · Between visits (light band; 3 items, `[icon]` left)

- **Message me directly.** A question, a symptom you're unsure about, a
  pharmacy problem — send it through the secure portal. I answer within
  1–2 business days, usually faster.
- **Results, explained.** Nothing sits unexplained in a portal. Every
  lab and report comes with my note: what it means, what we're doing
  about it, or why it's genuinely fine.
- **Refills, without games.** Prescriptions are managed at visits so
  you're not chasing renewals — and when something slips, message me
  and I'll sort it out.

## Decisions of record

The 90-day narrative is the spine ("sequences sell") · availability
leads with the why ("no good to anyone") · emergency strip is a
reusable component, identical everywhere · "away means slower, never
unaware" is canonical · no pricing duplication — §6 is a teaser with a
link. Approved amendment: emoji placeholders became `[icon]` markers
(design rule 1). Open item preserved: the up-to-90-minutes first-visit
promise stands unless the physician prefers "as long as it needs"; the
away-section carries the principle, not the three-month figure (the
agreement carries the number).
