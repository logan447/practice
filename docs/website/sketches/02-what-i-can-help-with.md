## Section order and copy

### 1 · Intro

> # What I can help with
> Most of what makes people feel better isn't exotic. It's ordinary
> medicine — done carefully, with enough time, by someone who knows
> you. Here's what that looks like in practice.

### 2 · The situations I'm built for (light band; 5 narrative cards, quoted-thought headlines; card 5 full width)

**"It's several things at once."** Blood pressure, weight, sleep,
energy, stress — each one manageable, together overwhelming. We'll sort
out what's urgent, what matters most, and what can wait — then work
through it in order, at a humane pace.

**"Something came back abnormal."** A lab result, a scan, a number in
your portal with no explanation attached. I'll go through it with you:
what it means, what it doesn't, what to do next, and what to watch.
Worry shrinks when things get explained.

**"I have conditions that deserve more than 15 minutes."** Diabetes,
blood pressure, cholesterol, thyroid — managed, not just refilled:
medications reviewed, complications watched for, numbers trending where
we agreed they should go.

**"Nothing's wrong — yet."** You want a doctor who knows your baseline,
watches your trends, keeps screening on schedule, and tells you the
truth about what matters for your health and what doesn't. Prevention
works best before it's urgent.

**"Honestly, my health and my life are tangled together."** (full
width) The stress is wrecking the sleep, the sleep is wrecking the
eating, the money worry sits under all of it — and your body is keeping
score. This is real medicine territory, and it's the kind I take most
seriously. We look at the whole picture, treat what needs treating, and
work the rest — one realistic step at a time. Where a counselor or
financial professional is the right tool, I'll help you find a good one
and stay involved.

### 3 · The everyday medicine (white; 6 category cards)

> Day to day, membership covers the full range of adult primary care:

- **Ongoing conditions** — High blood pressure, diabetes and
  prediabetes, cholesterol, thyroid, asthma, acid reflux, gout,
  migraine, and more
- **When you're sick** — Infections, colds that won't quit, UTIs,
  rashes, stomach trouble, injuries that don't need an ER, "is this
  serious?"
- **Prevention & screening** — Annual reviews, cancer screening on
  schedule, vaccines planned, risk assessment
- **Medications** — Reviewed as a whole: what's working, what's
  redundant, what's interacting; new prescriptions and refills handled
  without games
- **Mind & mood** — Depression and anxiety, adult ADHD, stress,
  burnout, and sleep problems including insomnia — treated seriously
  within primary care, referred wisely when needed
- **Lifestyle & habits** — Sleep, weight — including modern weight-loss
  medications where appropriate — exercise, nutrition, alcohol and
  tobacco: as medicine, not a lecture

> Plus: labs and imaging ordered and explained, specialist referrals
> with a real handoff, coordination when several doctors are involved —
> and the paperwork of being a patient handled without extra charges:
> disability and FMLA forms, letters, prior authorizations.

### 4 · What I don't do — and what happens instead (light band; equal visual weight to §3)

- **Emergencies.** Chest pain, stroke signs, severe injury — that's 911
  and the ER, always. Afterward, I help you make sense of what happened
  and what comes next.
- **Hospital care.** If you're admitted, the hospital's physicians run
  the admission. I stay informed and manage your landing when you're
  home.
- **Specialist-level conditions as the only doctor.** Some conditions
  need an oncologist, cardiologist, or rheumatologist leading. I refer
  well, co-manage, and keep the whole picture — I don't pretend to
  replace them.
- **Mental health beyond primary care's safe reach.** Serious
  psychiatric illness and addiction medicine beyond my scope get honest
  triage and a warm handoff, not improvisation.
- **Children.** Adults only (18+).

> If I'm ever not the right doctor for something, I'll tell you
> directly — that's part of what you're paying for.
