# Homepage

**Job:** the whole story in miniature. Within two minutes of calm
scrolling: what this is, whether it might be for them, what it costs,
that it works with their insurance, who the doctor is, the safe next
step. No section requires a click to understand the basics.

### 3 · What I do (white, essay column)

> ## Medicine, given room to work
>
> My job is to listen carefully, understand your whole situation —
> medical, and the life around it — figure out what's urgent, what
> matters most, and what can wait, and then work the plan with you over
> time. Visits are as long as they need to be. Between visits, you can
> message me and I'll answer — and every result comes with my
> explanation, not just a number.
>
> This isn't alternative medicine or a wellness program. It's
> evidence-based primary care with the one thing modern medicine rarely
> has: enough time.

### 4 · How it works, in four cards

1. **We talk first** — A free 30-minute video conversation. No
   pressure — if I'm not the right fit, I'll say so.
2. **You join if it fits** — $100/month. No sign-up fee, no contract.
   Cancel anytime.
3. **Care that fits you** — Mostly by video. I come to you when an exam
   truly needs it; clinic space when that's better.
4. **You see it working** — Your trends — blood pressure, labs, how you
   feel — visible to both of us, so we know it's worth it.

### 6 · Honest fit (white, two columns; "good fit" first on mobile)

**This practice is a good fit if…** you want a doctor who actually knows
you · your health feels tangled and you want help sorting it out ·
you're managing ongoing conditions and want them truly managed, not
just refilled · you'd rather prevent problems than chase them.

**It isn't if…** you need 24/7 physician access — I keep business hours
and take real time off (I'll always tell you how care works while I'm
away) · you're looking for emergency or hospital care — that's the ER's
job, and it never stops being the ER's job · you're under 18 — I care
for adults only.

> I'd rather be clear about this now than disappoint you later.
