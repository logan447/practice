The idea: space is the brand

The practice sells time and attention. The design expresses it as room — warm paper, one serious serif, a single deep green, real photography, and air. Everything a concierge-luxury, wellness, or corporate-health site would add is deliberately absent.

“It's several things at once.”

Blood pressure, weight, sleep, energy, stress — each one manageable, together overwhelming. We'll sort out what's urgent, what matters most, and what can wait — then work through it in order, at a humane pace.

EmergencyChest pain, stroke signs, severe breathing trouble, thoughts of self-harm: call 911 or go to the nearest ER. Never wait for me.
UrgentToday, and I'm not reachable: urgent care. Tell me after — I'll fold it into your care.
Everything elseMessage me — 1–2 business days, usually faster.

Why is it only $100 a month? What's the catch? −

The price works because the practice is small by design: no office building, no billing department, no staff, and a limited number of patients. The limits are stated rather than hidden — and when my panel is full, it is full. That is the whole catch.
