# Draft contract language: the Service Period

*Drafted 2026-10-25. **`NEEDS-COUNSEL`. I am not a lawyer and this is
not legal advice.** This is a starting point for a student-loan or
employment attorney to review, correct and adapt. Do not sign anything
based on it.*

---

## Why this language exists

The PSLF full-time test is an average of hours **per week across a
period**. The same 1,079 hours reports three different ways depending
on which period the certifier uses:

| Period certified | Hours per week | |
|---|---|---|
| The 35 service weeks | 30.8 | full time |
| 1 Feb to 14 Dec, elapsed | 24.0 | not full time |
| The full calendar year | 20.7 | not full time |

Nothing changes but the denominator. If the agreement does not define
the period, whoever completes the form will reasonably use the
employment dates and write 20.7.

**The language below must describe what actually happens.** If the
Physician in fact works scattered days across the whole year and no
weeks are genuinely designated as non-service, then calling it a
35-week Service Period is a label rather than a fact, and it will not
survive review. The designation has to be real, agreed in advance, and
honoured in practice.

## Schedule A — Service Period, Hours and Certification

**1. Contract Year.** *Contract Year* means the twelve consecutive
months beginning 1 June. The Physician's employment with the Clinic
continues throughout the Contract Year, including during Non-Service
Weeks.

**2. Service Period.** Within each Contract Year the Physician is
engaged for a *Service Period* of not fewer than thirty-five (35)
weeks. The Service Period is the aggregate of the Service Weeks
designated under paragraph 5 and is not required to be a continuous
calendar span.

**3. Non-Service Weeks.** The remaining weeks of the Contract Year are
*Non-Service Weeks*. In a Non-Service Week the Physician provides no
services and receives no compensation, and remains employed by the
Clinic.

**4. Scheduled hours.** During the Service Period the Physician is
scheduled for approximately eighty-three (83) clinic days at thirteen
(13) Clocked Hours each, being approximately 1,079 Clocked Hours,
which is an average of not less than thirty (30) Clocked Hours per
week across the Service Period.

**5. Designation and notice.** The Physician shall provide the Clinic
with his available days one calendar quarter in advance. The parties
shall designate the Service Weeks and Non-Service Weeks for each
Contract Year in writing before the Contract Year begins. For the
Contract Year beginning 1 June 2029 the Non-Service Weeks are the
weeks commencing (illustrative, from the 2029 model):

- 1 January 2029 (time off)
- 8 January 2029 (time off)
- 26 March 2029 (no coverage available)
- 2 April 2029 (time off)
- 9 April 2029 (no coverage available)
- 7 May 2029 (time off)
- 14 May 2029 (time off)
- 25 June 2029 (no coverage available)
- 23 July 2029 (time off)
- 30 July 2029 (time off)
- 6 August 2029 (time off)
- 10 September 2029 (no coverage available)
- 15 October 2029 (no coverage available)
- 19 November 2029 (time off)
- 26 November 2029 (time off)
- 17 December 2029 (time off)
- 24 December 2029 (time off)

**6. Clocked Hours.** *Clocked Hours* means all time the Physician
spends performing services for the Clinic, whether or not
patient-facing. Clocked Hours include clinical sessions, clinical
documentation, in-basket and patient message management, review and
communication of results, care coordination, telephone contact with
patients, and time worked beyond a scheduled session. Clocked Hours
are compensated at $50.00 per hour.

**7. Records and certification.** The Clinic shall maintain records of
the Physician's Clocked Hours. On the Physician's request the Clinic
shall complete any employment certification form the Physician
submits, and in doing so shall:

&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) identify the Service Period by its first and last dates
and its total number of weeks;

&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) report the Physician's average Clocked Hours per week
**computed over the Service Period** rather than over the Contract
Year or the dates of employment; and

&nbsp;&nbsp;(c) confirm that the Physician remained employed by the
Clinic throughout the Contract Year.

**8. Nature of employment.** The Physician is an employee of the
Clinic and is issued an IRS Form W-2. Nothing in this Schedule makes
the Physician an independent contractor.

## What counsel needs to opine on

1. **Does 34 CFR 685.219(b)(11)(i)(B) reach a clinic?** The text is
   "a minimum of 30 hours per week throughout a contractual or
   employment period of at least 8 months in a 12-month period, such
   as elementary and secondary school teachers and professors and
   instructors, in higher education". The Department's 2022 preamble
   calls that list "nonexhaustive". No published guidance applies it
   outside education.

2. **"Throughout" versus "average".** The regulation says a minimum of
   30 hours per week *throughout* the period. The PSLF form's
   instructions say *an average of* 30 hours per week for a minimum of
   8 months. Paragraph 4 above is drafted to the average. If the
   regulation's wording governs, each individual Service Week would
   have to reach 30 Clocked Hours, which this schedule does not do:
   most Service Weeks carry two clinic days, or 26 hours.

3. **Can a Service Period be non-continuous?** The regulation says
   "a ... period", singular. Paragraph 2 asserts it may be an
   aggregate. This is the single most load-bearing assumption in the
   whole arrangement.

4. **Is paragraph 7 enforceable, and will FCHC agree to it?** It
   commits the employer to a particular method of completing a federal
   form. An employer may reasonably decline, or may be unwilling to
   certify hours it does not separately track.

5. **Wage and hour.** Paragraph 6 counts documentation and in-basket
   time as compensated Clocked Hours. Confirm this is consistent with
   Virginia law and the Clinic's practice for other clinicians.

6. **Does the eight-month deeming cover all twelve months?** The
   regulation says the borrower "is deemed to have worked full time"
   and does not itself say "for 12 months". The form's instructions
   add that gloss.
