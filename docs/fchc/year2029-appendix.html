Each green square is a clinic day. I work only the weekdays my wife is home with our children, which is why there is no paid childcare anywhere in this year and no day on which we are both at work. Amber means the children are with me because she is on shift or post-call. A split square means both of us are home. Faded squares are weekends and my twelve weeks off, which are marked with where we would be.

Weeks off: Jan 1 to 13, Apr 1 to 7, May 6 to 19, Jul 22 to Aug 11, Nov 18 to Dec 1, Dec 16 to 31. Some weeks carry four clinic days, some carry one, and about six weeks a year carry none. The clinic would have each quarter three months ahead. This is a representative year built from my wife's usual pattern, not her actual 2029 schedule.
