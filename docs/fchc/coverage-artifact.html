How the hours get there. The forgiveness rule treats a borrower as full time who averages thirty hours a week across a contractual period of at least eight months. Eight months is 34.32 weeks, so the bar is 1,030 hours. Eighty-three days at twelve hours is 996, which lands at 29.0 and just misses. At twelve and a half clocked hours it is 1,037, which is 30.2. The whole gap is twenty-four minutes a day.
