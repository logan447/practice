How the hours get there. The forgiveness rule treats a borrower as full time who averages thirty hours a week across a contractual period of at least eight months. The contract here is 35 weeks, which is 8.05 months, so the bar is 1,050 hours. Eighty-three days at thirteen clocked hours is 1,079, which clears it with a little room. The floor is 12.65 hours a day, so thirteen leaves about a quarter of an hour of margin. Thirty-five weeks is the shortest whole-week period that still clears eight months: dropping the sixth empty week too would leave 34 weeks, a better average but only 7.82 months, which fails the minimum.

Why no childcare. I book a clinic day only when Jamie\'s schedule leaves her home with the children, so childcare never enters the plan. That is also what caps the year at 83 days: she is home about two weekdays a week, and no rearrangement finds more. Count the green squares week by week and the unevenness shows itself, some weeks three or four and most weeks two. Thirty-four weeks of the year carry at least one clinic day, which is where eight months\' worth of working weeks comes from.
