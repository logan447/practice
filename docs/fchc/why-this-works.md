# Why this works

*Draft summary statement for the FCHC conversation. House Style
applies. Figures from `tools/fchc_alternating.py` and
`tools/fchc_margin_frame.py`. The two inputs marked UNSET below are
not confirmed.*

---

I would work four days a week. Tuesday through Thursday every week,
plus Friday every other week. Twelve hours of scheduled patients each
day, forty weeks a year.

That comes to 141 clinic days and 1,692 paid hours. Over the year it
averages 32.5 hours a week. That meets the 30-hour standard for
Public Service Loan Forgiveness on hours I actually work. No leave
time has to count toward it.

At twenty patients a day, I see 2,820 patients a year. Visits run 36
minutes. The clinic books 24 minutes now.

At $62.25 an hour I cost $105,327 in wages, or $114,806 with payroll
taxes. If the clinic collects $100 a visit, those 2,820 visits bring
in $282,000. Overhead takes half. The clinic keeps $26,194.

Last year the clinic kept $21,133 across its whole operation. This
one role returns more than that.

My rate stays below the medical director's. I take no benefits. The
schedule is set a year in advance, so the clinic can staff it and
book patients against it.

At home, the pay covers childcare on the days my wife is at the
hospital, covers the second car, and still leaves $25,000 saved.

Two things have to be true. The clinic has to collect about $100 a
visit. And the 2,820 visits have to be patients we turn away now, not
patients the current providers would have seen anyway.
