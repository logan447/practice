# Practice One-Pager — Draft Copy

*Piece 2 of the outreach packet (architecture §3.2). Status: **draft for
review.** Updated 2026-11-12 for D-039 (Direct Lifestyle Medicine,
$99/month). Audience: the professional or gatekeeper who receives the
packet, not the patient. First person, House Style throughout (drafted
after the standard, so no inherited debts: no em dashes, short
sentences, ~5th-grade register with a professional reader in mind).
Bio facts match the approved About page and credentials table exactly.
Print only after the counsel pass and publishing gates.*

**Job:** the 60-second understanding, and the piece that stays on file
after the brochures are handed out. The reader should be able to judge
"is this useful to the people I serve" without visiting the website.

## Format

One letter sheet, duplex. Printed on demand (32 lb laser paper).
Front: the practice. Back: the doctor, how to start, contact.
Five labeled blocks per side, built for scanning. Block 1 on the front
carries the three services, because a reader who skims one block
should still come away with the shape of the practice. The old block 2
("what membership includes") and block 3 ("how care happens") merged
to make room for it, since the services block already names most of
what the fee covers; the block count is unchanged.

## Front

### Header

> **Direct Lifestyle Medicine**
> LOGAN DUBOSE, M.D. · [City], Texas

### Title block

> # The practice at a glance
>
> I run a small membership practice for adults. The focus is prevention
> and lifestyle medicine, with medical treatment when it is needed.
> Members pay one flat price: $99 a month. It covers all of my work as
> their doctor. Reduced-fee and no-fee memberships are available where
> cost is the barrier. I keep the practice small so every visit has
> enough time.
>
> Members do not need to have a primary care doctor. If they have one,
> I work alongside them.

### Block 1 · What the practice provides

> Three services, not three plans. Every member gets the first. The
> other two come in as their health calls for them.
>
> **Prevention and lifestyle medicine.** The foundation. Nutrition and
> metabolic health, activity, sleep, stress, social connection,
> alcohol and tobacco. Screenings and risk reduction. A written plan
> with goals we track.
>
> **Medical care and treatment.** Evaluation and diagnosis, management
> of conditions within my scope, medicines started and monitored, labs
> and imaging ordered, abnormal findings followed up.
>
> **Care coordination.** Working with a member's existing physicians,
> communicating with specialists, referrals, and help establishing
> other care when they need it.

### Block 2 · Who this may help

> - Adults who want to prevent problems, not just treat them
> - Adults working on weight, blood sugar, blood pressure, sleep,
>   stress, alcohol, or tobacco
> - Adults managing ongoing conditions who want close follow-up
> - Adults who rarely get in to see their own doctor
> - Adults who do not have a primary care doctor at all

### Block 3 · What membership includes, and how care happens

> All of this is covered by the monthly price, with no per-visit
> charges:
>
> - A full first assessment, and an individual written plan
> - Visits as often as care needs them, as long as they need to be
> - Direct messaging with me, answered within 1 to 2 business days
> - Every result reviewed and explained
> - Prescriptions I write, managed, and refills handled
> - Forms and paperwork handled
> - A full, unhurried review every year
>
> Most visits are by video. I make home visits when an exam calls for
> it. I use rented clinic space when that is better. There is no set
> visit schedule. Care follows what each person needs.

### Block 4 · Price and insurance

> $99 a month per adult. No sign-up fee. No contract. Cancel anytime.
>
> A reduced monthly fee is available based on a person's finances, and
> a small number of memberships carry no fee at all. The clinical care
> is the same at every level.
>
> Members keep their insurance and their other doctors. Insurance keeps
> paying for labs, imaging, medicines, specialists, and hospital care,
> as it does today. The membership is not insurance.
>
> I do not bill insurance, Medicare, or Medicaid. How a membership
> works for people on Medicare or Medicaid is still being settled with
> counsel. Please send them to me and I will tell them where it
> stands.

### Block 5 · The limits, stated plainly

> I keep business hours. I am not an emergency service. I treat within
> my scope and refer when something sits outside it, so this is not a
> promise to manage every condition a person has. I take real time away
> each year, with a clear plan for care while I am gone. I care for
> adults only. And when my panel is full, it is full.
